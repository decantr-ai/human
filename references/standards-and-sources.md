# Standards And Sources

H.U.M.A.N. Protocol should reference established standards and guidance rather than replacing them.

This page is a starting map, not an exhaustive bibliography.

## Accessibility

- W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/
- WCAG 2.2 Recommendation: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Guide: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/
- ARIA-AT: https://w3c.github.io/aria-at/
- EN 301 549: https://www.etsi.org/human-factors-accessibility/en-301-549-v3-the-harmonized-european-standard-for-ict-accessibility
- U.S. Section 508: https://www.section508.gov/

## Human-Centered Design And Usability

- ISO 9241-210, Human-centred design for interactive systems: https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html
- ISO 9241-11, Usability definitions and concepts: https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html

## Platform And Design Systems

- Apple Human Interface Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/
- Apple Accessibility: https://www.apple.com/accessibility/
- Android Accessibility: https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility
- Material Design: https://m3.material.io/
- Microsoft Inclusive Design: https://inclusive.microsoft.design/
- IBM Carbon Accessibility: https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/accessibility/overview/

## Web And Device Standards

- Open UI: https://open-ui.org/
- W3C Design Tokens Community Group: https://www.w3.org/community/design-tokens/
- WebXR Device API: https://www.w3.org/TR/webxr/
- OpenXR: https://www.khronos.org/openxr/
- Matter open-source SDK: https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip
- W3C Web of Things: https://www.w3.org/WoT/

- Google People + AI Guidebook: https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/
- Microsoft Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/
- C2PA Specifications: https://spec.c2pa.org/
