# Temporality

Experiences unfold over time. They are not only screens, components, or states.

A temporal model captures what happens before, during, after, repeatedly, under interruption, during recovery, and after trust changes.

## Guidance

- Describe phases, not just screens.
- Include interruption and recovery paths.
- Account for repeated use and fatigue.
- Include memory, history, expiration, and decay where relevant.
- Treat trust as something that changes through system behavior.
- Model error repair as part of the experience, not an edge case outside it.

## Useful Questions

- What does the user know before the interaction begins?
- What changes while the interaction is happening?
- What must be remembered or forgotten afterward?
- How does the experience behave after failure?
- What gets easier or harder after repeated use?
- What happens if the device, network, or AI system fails halfway through?
