# Evidence

Design claims need provenance.

H.U.M.A.N. Protocol distinguishes formal standards, legal requirements, platform guidance, research evidence, usability tests, accessibility tests, organizational policy, product decisions, design preferences, and hypotheses.

This distinction matters because not all claims should have the same authority.

## Guidance

- Label the source type behind important claims.
- Prefer primary standards and platform guidance for normative requirements.
- Mark assumptions and hypotheses clearly.
- Do not use vague "best practice" language when a stronger source is available.
- Do not treat AI-generated summaries as primary evidence.
- Keep open questions visible.

## Useful Questions

- Is this required by law, standard, policy, or product choice?
- What evidence supports the decision?
- What evidence is missing?
- What should be tested with users?
- What should be verified with assistive technology?
- What is only a hypothesis?
