# Agency

Agency is the ability to understand, decide, act, delegate, refuse, correct, undo, and seek recourse.

AI-mediated systems can increase agency by reducing effort and expanding capability. They can also reduce agency by hiding decisions, creating false confidence, automating without consent, or making alternatives hard to find.

## Guidance

- Make delegated action explicit.
- Require confirmation for consequential actions.
- Preserve edit, undo, pause, opt-out, and human-help paths where appropriate.
- State what the AI can and cannot do.
- Do not let convenience erase consent.
- Do not make the system's preferred path the only practical path.

## Useful Questions

- Who can act here?
- Who is accountable for the action?
- What can be undone?
- What requires explicit confirmation?
- What does the system infer, and can the user correct it?
- When does automation hand back to a human?
