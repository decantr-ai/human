# Social Meaning

Interfaces shape relationships between people.

This is especially visible in AI-mediated communication, collaboration, identity, care, education, employment, civic services, and customer support.

H.U.M.A.N. Protocol includes social meaning because a system can be usable and still distort authorship, empathy, accountability, consent, labor, or trust.

## Guidance

- Ask how the experience changes relationships, not only task completion.
- Include authorship and accountability where generated content is involved.
- Consider whether the system hides human labor.
- Consider whether automation makes people feel supported, surveilled, processed, or replaced.
- Treat tone, translation, summarization, and suggested replies as social interventions.

## Useful Questions

- Who is speaking?
- Who is being represented?
- Who is accountable?
- What labor is hidden or shifted?
- Does the system preserve dignity?
- Does the recipient know when AI mediation matters?
